01 The Studio

Designing Spaces.
Creating Experiences.

VASTU Architecture Design Studio is a professional design practice working across architectural design, interior solutions and construction. We approach each brief as a single, continuous story — from the first sketch of a plan to the final finish on site.

Every project begins with listening: how you live, how you work, how light moves through your day. From there we shape space with clarity and restraint — considered proportions, honest materials and detailing that rewards a closer look.

  • i Light & Proportion Rooms planned around daylight, ventilation and a calm sense of volume.
  • ii Material Honesty Stone, timber, plaster and metal used for what they are.
  • iii Buildable Design Drawings and details that translate cleanly to site and budget.
